## Featured projects

### Drinking Water Compliance

Analyzed reported health-based drinking water violations across eight Great Lakes states using R, DuckDB SQL, and Power BI. Includes statistical modeling, confidence intervals, reproducible SQL summaries, and automated R-versus-SQL consistency checks.

Among 11,202 systems with positive reported populations, 342 were flagged (3.05%). Reported-zero populations are documented separately, with 0 of 17 flagged.

### Email Campaign Incrementality

Analyzed a randomized email experiment involving 64,000 customers
using R and Power BI, with reproducible results and documented
validation.

- **Primary — purchase conversion:** Estimated increases of
  0.681 percentage points for the men's campaign and 0.311
  percentage points for the women's campaign versus no email.
- **Secondary — spending:** Estimated additional revenue of
  $0.770 and $0.424 per assigned customer, respectively,
  including customers who spent zero.

Includes individual 95% confidence intervals, Holm-adjusted
tests within each outcome, and an editable Power BI dashboard.
